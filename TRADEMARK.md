ManaWG logo are not part of that license. This policy says how you may use them.

- Running ManaWG for yourself, your household or your organization, unchanged or modified, under

- Saying that your project or service is "based on ManaWG" or "a fork of ManaWG".
- Using the name to refer to this project in articles, talks and reviews.

- Offering a public service to people outside your own household or organization under the name
  "ManaWG" or with the ManaWG logo, or in a way that suggests it is the official app.
- Using "ManaWG" in a domain name, app store listing or company name.
- Selling products or services under the name or logo.

If you run a public instance, give it a different name and logo. Ask at info@manawg.com if you are
unsure.
